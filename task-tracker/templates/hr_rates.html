Weekly hours

The workbook is emailed every Tuesday morning for the week that just ended. You can also download any week here — it is built fresh from the roster each time, so saving a rate above and downloading again picks up the new figure.

Hours come from the roster. Overtime (Day 1.5) is left empty for you to fill in, and a day off (ADV, FD, Sick, SOL) is credited as {{ absence_hours }} h of holiday pay with the code shown in column K.

An empty box leaves that rate as it was. Operators never see this page, and the rates do not appear anywhere else in the app.
